A SMALL NOTE ON DISCOVERY

Good surprises. Real sources.

Tagspin searches Wikimedia Commons by default, NASA’s image library for nasa.gov, Flickr’s public feed for flickr.com, and Openverse for other websites. Website filters match the image’s original page or image host in Openverse’s index; they don’t crawl an entire website. Availability and the number of matches depend on the source.

Your tag and source are sent to the search provider. Your spin history stays in this browser. Clearing browser data removes it; export a copy to keep it.

Images belong to their creators. Open an image to find its source and license before reusing it. The welcome collage uses photos from Unsplash.
